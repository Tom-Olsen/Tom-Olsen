          <div class="section-heading"><h2 id="research-title">The research</h2></div>
          <h3>General Relativistic Lattice Boltzmann Method for Radiation Transport</h3>
          <p>I translated abstract physical and mathematical models into high-performance, multithreaded C++ code. Alongside the numerical work, I developed a 3D visualization library in Unity using C# and HLSL to explore complex data structures.</p>
